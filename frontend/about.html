About Knuckle Sandwich

What We Do

Knuckle Sandwich is a web-based platform designed to help students and young people report incidents safely and efficiently. We provide a structured way to report concerns and connect with appropriate support resources.

Our Mission

To create a safe space where individuals can report incidents, receive acknowledgment, and be connected with the right resources to help them.

Key Features

  • Secure Reporting: Report incidents with your choice of anonymity
  • Smart Routing: Get connected to the right support service
  • Confidentiality: Your information is protected and encrypted
  • Confirmation & Resources: Receive support information after submission

Supported Routing Options

  • Parent
  • Teacher
  • School Administration
  • Local Support Services
  • Emergency Services (manual selection)
⚠️ DISCLAIMER: This is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, call 911.
